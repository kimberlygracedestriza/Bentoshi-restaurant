Our Story

Bentoshi started with a lunch box. In Japan, a bento is a full meal packed with care: rice, something grilled, something pickled, something sweet, each in its own corner.

We built a restaurant around that idea. The rice goes into the pot before we open, the fish is sliced to order, and every plate is sized so the whole family can share.
